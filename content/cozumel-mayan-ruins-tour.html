San Gervasio is Cozumel’s main Mayan archaeological site — a sacred complex associated with Ixchel. It is a genuine cultural stop, but it is not equivalent to major mainland ruins. Shore excursions explore temple areas and island history, sometimes paired with beach time on the return leg.

Best Adventure Tour Plan a return buffer

ATV days on Cozumel are for passengers who want an active land outing rather than a beach lounge. Routes, vehicle types and age rules vary by operator — treat duration and inclusions as package-specific, and expect heat, dust and uneven surfaces.

  • ✓Riding time is usually measured in hours rather than a full day, plus briefing and transfers — confirm with the operator.
  • ✓A valid driving licence is commonly required; minimum ages vary.
  • ✓Moderate to high effort — closed-toe shoes and clothes you do not mind getting dusty help.
  • ✓Not a substitute for reef time if underwater scenery is your priority.

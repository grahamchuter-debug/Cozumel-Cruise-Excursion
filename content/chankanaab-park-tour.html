Chankanaab National Marine Park is a popular cruise-day stop south of the piers — lagoon and beach facilities, a house reef snorkel zone, and optional animal programs depending on what is offered on the day. Journey time depends on which terminal you use and traffic.

  • ✓Often fits within a half-day or longer call once transfers are included — confirm duration with the operator.
  • ✓House reef snorkel is commonly available; gear hire and add-ons vary by package.
  • ✓A short taxi or organised transfer from the main cruise terminals for many ships.
  • ✓Lockers, showers and food outlets are typically available inside the park.

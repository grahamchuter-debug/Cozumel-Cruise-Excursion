Cozumel sits on the Mesoamerican Reef — the Caribbean’s great underwater draw for cruise passengers. Boat snorkel days often visit sites such as Palancar, Columbia Reef and the El Cielo sandbar. Sightings and visibility change with conditions; treat wildlife as a possibility, not a promise.

  • ✓Typical boat formats leave time for multiple water stops within a cruise call — confirm duration with the operator.
  • ✓Morning departures are often preferred when seas are calmer on the leeward coast.
  • ✓Basic swimming comfort helps; ask about flotation support if you are a nervous snorkeller.
  • ✓Reef-safe sunscreen or a rash guard is kinder to the marine park environment.
